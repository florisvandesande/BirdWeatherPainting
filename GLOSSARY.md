# BirdWeather Painting

This glossary defines the terms used by the BirdWeather Painting experience: a visual display of recent bird detections and the artwork that represents them.

## Views and data

**Single view**:
The display that presents one most recently detected bird with its common and scientific names.
_Avoid_: Single painting, latest screen

**Collage**:
The display that arranges the unique species detected today into one composed field-guide image.
_Avoid_: Grid, gallery

**Daily species details**:
The information surface opened from the `i` control that lists every unique species detected today, including species that are outside the visible collage limit.
_Avoid_: Bird list, species popup

**Detection count**:
The number of detections for one species during the current day and selected detection source.
_Avoid_: Global count, sightings total

## Artwork

**Artwork**:
The local historical bird illustrations and branch illustrations used by the painting and collage views.
_Avoid_: Plates, Fugleramme folder

**Perch**:
A branch illustration shown while detection data is loading or when the selected source has no bird to display.
_Avoid_: Empty branch, loading image
