# Fugleramme Classic artwork

The Fugleramme bird illustrations and perches under this directory are adapted
from the Fugleramme Classic collection. Fugleramme contributors prepared the
collection from the original works listed in the [upstream attribution
ledger](https://github.com/arnegiacomo/fugleramme/blob/main/assets/artwork/classic/ATTRIBUTION.md).
Each entry in `catalog.json` retains its collection source tag and direct
original-source URL. The painting links to both the Fugleramme attribution
ledger and the individual source record.

The collection and these adapted images are provided under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
The adapted images remain under that licence.

Changes for this app: each image was resized to a maximum edge of 600 pixels,
converted from WebP to JPEG for older Safari, and prepared in light and dark
versions. The versions are flattened over the app's `#f0ece6` light and
`#171512` dark paper colors. Before JPEG encoding, smooth, pale pixels close to
transparent edges were softened toward the matching paper color while local
color variation was preserved to protect feathers, twigs, and needles. The
source metadata was not embedded in the JPEG files; the source record for each
image is preserved in `catalog.json`.
